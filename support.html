How do I reset my password?

Go to your LeapFILE URL (for example https://yourcompanyname.leapfile.net), click Employee Login, then "Forgot your password?" — or visit fileexchange.leapfile.net/app/login. If your company uses Active Directory authentication, contact your IT department directly.

This account has been disabled

To maintain account security and privacy, LeapFILE customer service cannot enable your account. Contact your internal LeapFILE account administrator directly. If you don't know who your administrator is, email support@leapfile.com and we'll point you to the right person.

Can I access or resend an expired or complete transfer?

Once a transfer is marked complete or expired, files are permanently deleted from the LeapFILE server and options like Forward, Resend, and Cancel will be inactive. Contact the sender to send the files again, or use Repository or Portal features to store files longer term.

What is the maximum file size?

The maximum file size limit is account dependent but can be increased by contacting your account representative. Browsers have a default upload limit of 4GB using the Enhanced HTML 5 Uploader. The LeapFILE Desktop client (Outlook compatible) has no file size restrictions.

Security session expired

When you visit your LeapFILE URL, it may redirect to a path containing /fts/drop/custom/Index.jsp. Do not bookmark or share that redirected URL — it includes a session ID that expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Always provide users with your clean URL (e.g. https://mycompany.leapfile.net).
